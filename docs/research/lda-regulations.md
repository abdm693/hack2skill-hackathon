# LDA residential building regulations: compliance reference (Lahore, Punjab)

- **Compiled:** 2026-10-16
- **Scope:** single-family houses (not apartment buildings) in areas controlled by the Lahore Development Authority (LDA): LDA schemes and LDA-approved private housing schemes. The file also covers the LDA rules for established built-up areas and predominantly open areas, and it compares LDA with other Punjab regulators, DHA Lahore and Bahria Town.
- **Primary instrument:** the LDA Building & Zoning Regulations 2019 (LDA B&ZR 2019), notified under No. LDA/C&I/3798 on 11-09-2019 and amended several times since.
- **Text used for the current numbers:** the LDA's own consolidated PDF, `09-02-2026-amended-building-regulations-2019-with-amendment.pdf`, published on lda.gop.pk (the PDF was created on 2026-02-09). Its running header still reads "with all amendments approved till 28-01-2020", but the body includes later amendments, such as the 2022 Office Order and the 2025 changes.

## (a) Summary

1. **No province-wide code has replaced the LDA B&ZR 2019.**
   - The Punjab Spatial Planning Authority Act 2025 (s.22) keeps each authority's own regulations in force until rules are notified under the Act. None were found.
   - The current text is the LDA's own consolidated version of 09-02-2026 (**VERIFIED**).
   - The LDA Governing Body approved further amendments on 2026-08-19, but their text has not been published yet (N6, **SECONDARY**).
   - Search results for a "Punjab Unified Building Rules 2025" refer to **Indian** Punjab and do not apply here.
2. **The marla is defined differently in Lahore.**
   - In Lahore District a marla is **225 sq ft** (20.91 m²), so 1 kanal = 20 marla = 4,500 sq ft (LDA definition, **VERIFIED**).
   - Elsewhere the definition depends on the regulator: 272 sq ft (LDA text for other districts, GDA), 272.25 sq ft (FDA, traditional revenue measure) or 270 sq ft (MDA, urban).
   - Always convert using the controlling body's own definition (§12.4).
3. **Setbacks in approved schemes [v2026].** These are front (building line) / rear / side distances:
   - Less than 10 marla: 5 / 5 ft, with no side space.
   - 10–30 marla: 10 / 7 ft, plus a 5 ft side space on **one** side.
   - Over 30 marla to under 2 kanal: 10 / 7 ft, plus 5 ft on **both** sides.
   - 2 to under 4 kanal: 20 / 10 / 10 ft (both sides).
   - 4 kanal and above: 20 / 13 / 13 ft (both sides). This band is new since 2020.
4. **Ground coverage and FAR.** Coverage is 80 % (under 5 marla), 75 % (5 to under 10 marla), 70 % (10 marla to under 1 kanal), 65 % (1 kanal to 30 marla), 60 % (over 30 marla to under 2 kanal) and 55 % (2 kanal and above). The matching FAR is 1:2.4, 1:2.3, 1:2.8, 1:2.6, 1:2.4 and 1:2.2. **The basement counts in FAR.**
5. **Height.** The maximum is **38 ft** for plots under 10 marla and **45 ft** for 10 marla and above. Height is measured from the crown of the road to the top of the parapet. A stair tower (mumty) up to **9 ft 6 in**, water tanks, lift heads and chimneys are excluded from this height [v2026].
6. **Storeys.** The maximum is **3 storeys, excluding the basement, for every plot size** [v2026, clause 2.7.1(a): "No Building Plan shall be approved more than 3 storeys"]. This is a major change: the 2020 text allowed **4 storeys for 10 marla and above**.
7. **Habitability.**
   - Habitable room: at least 100 sq ft with a minimum width of 8 ft.
   - Kitchen: at least 50 sq ft with a minimum width of 6 ft.
   - Ceiling and clear storey height: at least 9 ft 6 in.
   - Windows: glazing of at least 8 % of the floor area, half of it openable.
   - Bathroom or WC: an external opening of at least 2 sq ft.
   - Stairs (up to 3 storeys): at least 3 ft 6 in wide, risers of at most 7.5 in, treads of at least 10 in, and no more than 15 risers per flight.
   - **No minimum** is set for toilet size, corridor width or handrail height.
8. **Projections.** A sunshade (chajja) may project up to 3 ft where the open space is at least 10 ft, and up to 1 ft 6 in otherwise. No balconies, bay windows or porches may project into the mandatory open spaces. The boundary wall may be at most 7 ft above plinth level.
9. **Structure [v2026].** "No pillar construction is allowed for residential building/house/farm house"; "concealed columns" for strengthening are allowed. By contrast, the 2020 text **required** a frame structure for houses of 4 or more storeys.
10. **Water and drainage.** Overhead and underground water tanks are required for 3 marla and above. A septic tank is required for every house. A rainwater-harvesting storage tank is required for 5 marla and above, and a recharge well for 15 marla and above. A greywater treatment plant is required for **1 kanal and above** (S1), whereas news reports (**SECONDARY**) say "larger than 10 marla".
11. **Approval process.**
    - The plan must be prepared, stamped and signed by an architect registered with the Pakistan Council of Architects and Town Planners (PCATP) and enlisted with the LDA.
    - The LDA must decide within **21 days** in approved schemes and **30 days** in controlled areas. If it does not, the owner may start building after paying the fee and filing an affidavit.
    - The plan-scrutiny fee is **Rs 15 per sq ft** of covered area [v2026].
    - LDA-registered architects or town planners may carry out completion inspections for houses.
    - Online submission has run through the LDA e-Khidmat / One Window system since August 2024, although the system has had outages. The Punjab e-Biz portal has handled commercial plans since November 2025.
12. **Other regulators (§12).**
    - RDA, FDA and GDA adopted the LDA 2019 text as it stood on 28-01-2020, so they still allow 4 storeys at 10 marla and above.
    - MDA has its own 2020 regulations: 38/48 ft measured to the roof, with the parapet extra, and boundary walls up to 8 ft.
    - DHA Lahore's August 2026 bylaws cap houses at **39 ft** (basement, ground and first floor only) and set their own clear spaces. For example, 5 marla: 5/3/3 ft; 1 kanal: 15 ft 9 in front, with 5 ft 4½ in rear and on both sides.
    - Bahria Town Lahore's bylaws are not published online.
13. **Main caveats.**
    - The 1.5 × right-of-way height formula of November 2025 appears in S1 only for "permissible uses in residential area" (clause 3.9.1 note c). The 38/45 ft house caps are unchanged in the S1 text.
    - The content of the August 2026 amendments is unknown.
    - The LDA sets **no** minimum for toilet size, corridor width or handrail height, so these checks can only be advisory.

Notes to table 2.2.3 (S1):

- (a) "Basement is included in FAR."
- (b) A structural stability certificate and structural drawings and calculations are needed for buildings above 38 ft, vetted by a structural engineer on the LDA panel. The note still says "(4 Storeys)", which is left over from the old 4-storey limit.
- (c) After amalgamation, the rules for the combined plot apply.
- (d) The table applies to new and rebuilt plans only.
- (e) "For parking provision, a storey must contain at least a habitable room, a latrine, bath room and a kitchen."

**Definitions (S1 §1.1, VERIFIED):**

- **FAR** = "aggregate covered area … (excluding the area under covered parking) … divided by the total area of the plot".
- **Ground coverage** = "percentage of the plot area that can be covered at the ground floor".
- **Covered area** excludes ground-level courtyards, gardens, uncovered pools, water tanks, boundary walls, gates, uncovered staircases, watchman booths, pump houses, sump tanks and transformers.
- Whether a *parking* basement is excluded under the FAR definition or included under note (a) is unclear. See open questions.

**How the MDA B&ZR 2020 (Multan) differs from LDA**

- **Setbacks.** Under 10 marla: 5/5 ft. 10 marla to 1 kanal: 10/7 ft with 5 ft on one side. Above 1 kanal to under 2 kanal: 10/7 ft with 5 ft on **both** sides (LDA: both sides only above 30 marla). 2 kanal and above: 20/10/10 ft.
- **FAR** is the same as LDA. **No ground-coverage % is given** for houses.
- **Height:**
  - Up to **38 ft** (under 10 marla) or **48 ft** (10 marla and above), with 3 or 4 storeys.
  - Height is measured "from the crown of the road to the roof top (finished floor level of roof)". The parapet is *extra*: at least 2 ft 9 in, at most 5 ft 6 in.
- **Building details:**
  - Plinth 1 ft 6 in.
  - Porch 12–20 ft.
  - Sunshade at most 1 ft 6 in.
  - Boundary wall at most **8 ft above the road crown**.
  - A basement must keep the mandatory open spaces, and at least 5 ft where none is defined.
  - Farm-house coverage 40 %.
- **Home office:** it needs committee approval **and** a land-use conversion fee.
- **Marla:** 270 sq ft for urban plots and 272.25 sq ft for rural ones.

## (d) Open questions and uncertainties

### LDA: version and currency

1. **The August 2026 amendments are unknown.** The Governing Body approved "amendments to the LDA Building and Zoning Regulations, 2019" on 2026-08-19 (N6), but no text has been published. Before using this file in production, re-check https://lda.gop.pk/downloads for a consolidated PDF newer than 09-02-2026.
2. **There is no official amendment log.** S1's running header still reads "with all amendments approved till 28-01-2020". We identified the changes since 2020 by comparing S1 with S2 word by word, so the dates and notification numbers of several changes are not confirmed:
   - the 3-storey cap;
   - the pillar ban;
   - the 4-kanal band;
   - the mumty exclusion;
   - the 20-ft floor height;
   - the greywater rule.

   News dates the pillar ban and greywater rule to about April 2025 (N2, N3).
3. **Transitional rule.** Plans are "processed as per regulations which were applicable on the date of submission", and revised plans keep the FAR, height and coverage already approved (S1 general instructions iii-iv). Houses sanctioned under v2020 may therefore legitimately have 4 storeys.

### LDA: unclear rules

4. **Scope of the 1.5 × ROW formula.** S1 places it under §3.9.1(c) for "permissible uses in residential area". News (N1) says it replaces "the uniform 48-foot cap in residential areas". It is unconfirmed whether it can raise or lower the 38/45 ft limits for houses. Current treatment: it does not apply to houses.
5. **Greywater threshold.** S1 says a greywater treatment *plant* for 1 kanal and above; news says greywater *tanks* for plots above 10 marla. The design specification ("Stereotype Design") in S1 is a diagram that is not reproduced here.
6. **Mumty.** It is not stated whether the 9 ft 6 in limit is measured from the roof slab or the parapet top. No maximum area or setback from the façade is specified. CAA criteria apply near airports.
7. **Parapet.** The only rule (a minimum of 2 ft 9 in) sits in the "Shops" sub-clause 5.2.2(b). Applying it to houses is our inference, and no maximum is given.
8. **FAR and parking basements.** The FAR definition excludes "the area under covered parking", but note (a) to §2.2.3 says "Basement is included in FAR". It is unclear whether a parking-only basement in a house counts.
9. **Basements.** No limit on the number of basements and no minimum plot size was found for houses. The 2025 news that "basements and underground parking [are] optional" concerns high-rises.
10. **The structural note is stale.** §2.2.3 note (b) still says "above 38’ (4 Storeys)" although all houses are now capped at 3 storeys. A 3-storey house over 38 ft (allowed from 10 marla, up to 45 ft) still needs vetted structural drawings.
11. **Floor height of 20 ft (§2.7(xi)).** The same text appears in the commercial chapter. It is unclear whether the LDA intends it for houses; the overall 38/45 ft cap still binds.
12. **Approval deadline.** §10.4.1 says 45 days, while §10.13 says 21 days (approved schemes) or 30 days (controlled areas). Use §10.13, the later and more specific clause.
13. **Compounding.** Mandatory-space violations are "non-compoundable" (§2.7.1(b)), yet Appendix C and §10.11.1(A) allow composition with a neighbour's NOC. The 2021 draft (S4 item 13) flagged the same contradiction.
14. **Band edges.**
    - The water-tank rows ("5-Marla", "Upto 7 Marla", "7 Marla to 1 Kanal") and the rainwater bands (e.g. 9–10 marla, 20–22 marla) have gaps or overlaps.
    - The 15 % plot-size tolerance proposed in the 2021 draft was not adopted.
    - The engine should flag plots within about 0.5 marla of a band edge for manual review.
15. **Room over the porch** is granted "in case of sites having a requirement of minimum 5-ft side space". It is unclear for plots of 2 kanal and above, where the side space is 10 or 13 ft.
16. **Rooftop gardening.** It is unclear whether the "Roof Top Active Gardening not less than 50 %" requirement for completion certificates (§10.9.3(e)) applies to houses.
17. **Items in setbacks.** §2.1.1(c), which allows septic tanks and swimming pools in mandatory spaces, is written in the built-up-areas section. Whether it applies to approved schemes is assumed, not stated.

### LDA: gaps to fill elsewhere

18. **No LDA numbers exist** for minimum toilet or bathroom size, corridor width, stair handrail height or balcony guard height. Possible fallbacks are the Building Code of Pakistan (Fire Safety Provisions 2016, which is based on NFPA 101) and the National Reference Manual (NRM). Neither was retrieved or verified here. The engine should mark these checks as "advisory".
19. **Corner plots.** The LDA B&ZR does not say whether a building line is required on the second road frontage. Scheme layout plans or LDA practice may require one.
20. **Private schemes.** LDA-approved private schemes may add scheme-level covenants, such as elevation control under §5.1.7, uniform boundary walls or gate designs. These need per-scheme data.
21. **Online submission.** The public URL or login for the LDA's residential online plan system was not found. It is unclear whether residential plans can now be filed on e-Biz Punjab, which is documented only for commercial plans.

### DHA Lahore and Bahria Town

22. **DHA first-floor percentage.** It is unclear whether the % applies to the *permissible* or the *as-built* ground floor. The 2021 text said "permissible"; DHA-1 just says "of GF".
23. **DHA porches.** On 5–10 marla plots, the exact geometry of a porch over the clear space is unclear ("maximum depth … 18 feet … if clear space is covered").
24. **DHA gaps and internal conflicts.**
    - Gaps: main-gate width, chamfer size and stair width are not stated, and the roof parapet height is only implied (a "3 feet parapet wall" in Reg 65).
    - Conflicts: the sunshade definition says 2 ft but Reg 27a(4) allows 30 in; approval time is 20 working days (website) vs 21 (Reg 16); prints required are 5 vs 7.
25. **DHA phases.** No phase-specific variations (Rahbar, 9 Town, Prism, etc.) were found. DHA-1 says the 39 ft cap applies "for all phases".
26. **Bahria Town Lahore.** The official bylaws or SOPs are not online. Ask the Bahria Town Lahore Design Wing for its current SOP. It is unknown whether the group's 8th-revision SOPs (Rawalpindi) or any LDA rules apply in Lahore.

### Jurisdiction and other Punjab bodies

27. **LDA jurisdiction outside Lahore District.** Control of Kasur, Sheikhupura and Nankana Sahib is unsettled: the LDA Act was extended to Lahore Division in 2013, a 2021 ordinance limited it to the Lahore metropolitan area, and a caretaker government later partly restored it (SECONDARY). Confirm which body controls the plot before applying LDA rules there. If the LDA does, a marla is 272 sq ft.
28. **Subdivision conflict.** The LDA Private Housing Schemes Rules 2014, rule 29, allow a plot of 1 kanal or more to be split into plots of at least 10 marla. The B&ZR §5.1.4 allows subdivision only of plots of 2 kanal or more, into plots of at least 1 kanal. It is unclear which prevails in LDA-approved private schemes.
29. **Adopting authorities.**
    - It is unknown whether RDA, FDA or GDA have adopted any LDA amendment made after January 2020, such as the 3-storey cap or the pillar ban.
    - The gazette dates for the RDA and GDA versions were not found.
    - MDA's website lists both its 2007 manual (amended 2016) and its 2020 regulations.
30. **Marla ambiguity.**
    - FDA defines a marla as 225 sq ft in Faisalabad District, but its acre clause implies 272.25.
    - MDA uses 270 sq ft for urban plots, and GDA uses 272.
    - No official Board of Revenue definition was found online.
31. **PHATA schemes.** Which body approves house plans in PHATA schemes is unverified.
32. **Local-government areas.** Outside the development authorities, each local government frames its own building bye-laws under the Punjab Local Government Act 2025, and none were compiled here. The engine needs per-municipality rulesets for those plots.
33. **Watch list.**
    - Any regulations under the **Punjab Spatial Planning Authority Act 2025**, including its "automated zoning reports" portal, could supersede authority-level rules.
    - Section 32 of the Punjab Local Government Act 2025 allows development-authority functions to move to local governments.
34. **Cantonments.** Building bylaws for the Lahore and Walton Cantonments were not researched.
