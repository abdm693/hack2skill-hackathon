## Slide 2 · Brief about the idea

**Chip next to the title:** Theme 5 · Future of Work & Enterprise Productivity. This is the only chip; delete the industry chip and the proof chips.

**Headline:**
> From a conversation to a bylaw-compliant 3D home you can walk through, in minutes.

**Sub-line, small and muted:** Naqsha (نقشہ) is Urdu for "house plan".

### Left column: The problem

1. **Design takes weeks.** Every draft and every change is drawn by hand.
2. **Most families never see their home in 3D,** let alone walk through it. They commit to building from flat drawings.
3. **Compliance needs scarce experts.** Bylaw mistakes often show up only when the authority refuses the plan.
4. **Feedback is scattered.** Owners, family, architects and builders give it in different places, so every round of changes is slow.

### Right column: The solution

**Lead line:** Naqsha works like an experienced architect you can talk to.

1. **Designs in minutes, by conversation.** Describe the plot and the family in plain words. Naqsha drafts the concepts, the floor plans and the full 3D home.
2. **Compliant from the start.** Every plan is checked against the local authority's bylaws, and Naqsha fixes what fails.
3. **High-fidelity 3D you can walk through.** Furnished inside, finished outside, and viewable from any angle, by day or night.
4. **Comments that get addressed.** Pin a note anywhere as you walk. Naqsha makes the change, or names the rule that stops it.
5. **One project for everyone.** Owners, family, architects and builders review and comment together.
6. **Change it by asking.** "Make the kitchen bigger" gives you a new, checked version.

**Visual:** ONE walkthrough image: the furnished lounge from the live app (`assets/walk/walk-lounge-39.jpg`, or a cleaner frame from the walk footage). Caption: "Walking through a design in the live app".

**Footer line, small:** Prototype: Lahore (LDA) bylaws. Other authorities' rules load the same way.

**Leave out:** the tech footer line, the sources line, the "Why it matters" line, the three-image strip and both proof chips. The aim is a calm slide with two clear columns and one image.

## Slide 3 · Opportunities

**Header chip:** Compared with how homes are designed today

### How different is it from any of the other existing ideas?

**Lead, bold:** No product today takes a family from a conversation to a compliant 3D home they can walk through, comment on and change.

- **Architect or draftsman:** weeks per design, in 2D, with every change redrawn by hand.
- **3D renders:** ordered separately, they take days and are still pictures you can't walk through.
- **AI image and floor-plan tools:** a picture or a 2D layout, with no bylaw checks, no walkthrough and no shared project.

### How will it be able to solve the problem?

- **Weeks of drafting:** a complete design in minutes, from one conversation.
- **No 3D:** every design is a high-fidelity 3D home you can walk through.
- **Scarce compliance experts:** bylaw checks are built in, so the architect stamps a set that is already checked.
- **Scattered feedback:** comments pinned in the model, one shared project, and changes made by asking.

### USP of the proposed solution

**Big line, cobalt:** An experienced architect you can talk to.

- AI agents that design the home, check their own work against the bylaws and fix it.
- Immersive, high-fidelity 3D, inside and out, in the browser.
- Collaboration and changes in plain language, all in one place.
- Rules as data: LDA in the prototype, and any authority's bylaws can be added.

## Slide 4 · List of features offered by the solution

**Hero banner across the top, the most important feature:**
> **From your requirements to a complete, bylaw-compliant 3D walkthrough of your home, in minutes.**

**Feature grid: 12 cards, each a short title plus one line. No technology names.**

1. **Design by conversation:** describe your plot, family and wishes in plain words; Naqsha asks only for what's missing.
2. **Concepts by day and night:** four photoreal exterior options for your plot, each also shown at night.
3. **Compliant floor plans:** every floor is checked against the authority's bylaws and livable-home rules; Naqsha fixes what fails and reviews its own drawing.
4. **High-fidelity 3D, inside and out:** furnished rooms, finished facades, any angle, and cut-away floors.
5. **Immersive walkthrough:** walk room to room, jump to any room, and take the stairs up to the roof.
6. **Night lighting and photoreal renders:** light the house at night, or turn any view into a photoreal image in seconds.
7. **Comments where you stand:** pin a note on any wall, window or room as you walk.
8. **Comments addressed for you:** Naqsha makes each change, or names the rule that stops it and offers another way.
9. **Change it by asking:** type a request in plain language and get a new, checked version.
10. **Collaborate on one project:** owners, family, architects and builders together; client links need no account.
11. **Versions and drawings:** every version is kept with what changed, in feet; there is a 1:100 print set and a compliance report.
12. **Bylaws for your area:** LDA in the prototype; other authorities' rules load as data.

**Bottom band: who it helps** (one line each, compact):
- **Families** see, walk and shape their home before they build.
- **Architects** deliver a checked design in the first meeting.
- **Construction companies** sell and build from designs buyers can walk through.
